Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

4D Ages is a private baby book. This page explains what it keeps, where, who can see it, and how to remove it.

What the app keeps

  • Your account: your email address, and your name if you add one. If you sign in with Google, Google tells us your email address and name.
  • Your child's book: what you add: your child's name, birth date and gender; photos, videos and voice notes; stories, milestones, firsts and lasts; heights, weights and head measurements; health notes; family members you add; and comments and hearts.
  • On your phone: a copy of all of this, so the app works without a connection.

The app has no advertising and no tracking, and we never sell or share your data.

Where it is kept

In your account with our database provider, Supabase, in the European Union (Frankfurt). Photos and videos are in private storage: they are never public and can't be found by searching.

Who can see it

Only you, and the people you invite: at most one other parent, who sees and adds to the whole book, and any family members you invite to view only the posts with the people you choose. Nobody else, including us, looks at your book.

Photo access

The app only sees the photos and videos you pick. It never scans your phone's gallery.

Keeping and deleting

Your book is kept until you delete it. You can delete a post, a child's book or your whole account at any time: in the app (menu, Settings, "Remove my data and delete my account"), or on this page. Deleting your account removes your books and their photos from our database straight away. A book you share with the other parent is handed over to them instead, because it is their book too.

Children

The app is for parents. The information about a child is added by their parent, who controls who sees it and can delete it at any time.

Your rights

You can see, correct, download and delete your data in the app at any time. For anything else, contact us below.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue on the app's page.
