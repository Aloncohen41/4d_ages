Music in 4D Ages videos

Every track here is in the public domain (CC0 1.0 Universal, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
They come from the FreePD.com collection of public domain music. The site has closed; the collection is kept
at https://archive.org/details/freepd (a CC0 mirror is at https://archive.org/details/abook-music-freepd-cc0).

No credit is required, and videos made with them can be shared anywhere. We list the original pieces anyway:

  stars.m4a      "Shining Stars"        (FreePD, Romantic)
  ukulele.m4a    "Ukulele Song"         (FreePD, Upbeat)
  garden.m4a     "Magic in the Garden"  (FreePD, Scoring)
  celebrate.m4a  "Celebration"          (FreePD, Misc)

Each was shortened to under two minutes, levelled in loudness, given a gentle fade in and out, and saved as
96 kbps AAC, so the app stays small. The videos loop a track when they run longer.
